## Agenda

1. Presentation of the project

## Notes

- **aLinha** — everything has to be aligned. The project exists to help organisations
  guarantee information-security compliance.
- Every presentation's second slide answers one sentence: *"our project is … and does …"*.
- Questions the project must be able to answer:
  1. What is the project?
  2. What does it do?
  3. Why not simply hire someone to handle compliance? — Cost: it is high, because the
     role needs credibility, and credibility is hard to earn.
  4. How do we build a tool that empowers a CISO?
- The project's chatbot removes the need for a dedicated person on site, lowers the cost and
  adds a layer of abstraction over the regulatory language.
- We follow an existing model that does **not** automate 100%: someone has to remain
  accountable.
- Target: comply with **NIS2**.
- **Tally** is a good reference for the interaction style.
