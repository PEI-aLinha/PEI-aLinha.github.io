# Notes

- Legal requirements:

  - GDPR: Inês.

    - Data purpose: which data is collected and why.

    - How data anonymisation/pseudonymisation will be ensured.

    - How the right to be forgotten is implemented (deleting data on request).

    - Privacy policy: which information is shared with third parties.

    - Security measures to protect the data (encryption, access control).

    - If the project collects personal information, justify the need for each data item and describe the deletion mechanism.

  - Licensing of third-party software and open-source libraries used in the project: Tomás.

    - Check for conflicts between licences.

    - Ensure the licences allow the intended use.

  - Standards and regulations in the project's domain: Íris.

    - Each sector has its own regulations that can directly affect the project.

    - Identify the standards that apply to our sector and explain how the project complies with or adapts to them.

  - Protecting the software creators: Martim.

    - Clear usage rules for the product/service that protect the creators from legal action and define the limits of liability:

      - Terms of service (what is allowed and forbidden).

      - Liability disclaimers.

      - Legal jurisdiction (which law applies in case of dispute).

      - Changes to the terms (how and when they can be modified).

- Usability and digital accessibility:

  - Who can and cannot use our software: everyone.

  - How each screen creates different barriers for each profile: everyone.
